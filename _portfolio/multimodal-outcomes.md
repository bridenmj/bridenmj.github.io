## The research question

Can complementary clinical modalities be combined to predict medical outcomes without full-scale model fine-tuning?

This ongoing project develops a parameter-efficient multimodal generative model integrating wound images, RNA measurements, and text. It explores how efficient fine-tuning can adapt large pretrained models to limited clinical datasets while keeping the resulting system inspectable and practical to evaluate.

## What the case study will show

- How each modality is represented and aligned.
- Where parameter-efficient fine-tuning fits into the architecture.
- How multimodal performance compares with single-modality baselines.
- How uncertainty, missing modalities, and failure cases are evaluated.
