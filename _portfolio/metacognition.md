## The research question

How can an EEG model learn useful shared representations without erasing meaningful variation between subjects?

This work combines subject-aware supervised contrastive learning with deep fusion networks for confidence analysis. Related research extends explainable contrastive fusion methods to anxiety-level analysis.

## Research outputs

- *Towards Metacognition: Incorporating Subject-Aware Supervised Contrastive Learning With Deep Fusion Networks to Learn Confidence* — CVPR 2022 NeuroVision.
- *Subject-Aware Explainable Contrastive Deep Fusion Learning for Anxiety Level Analysis* — in submission.
- [Code on GitHub](https://github.com/bridenmj/Metacognition)

The broader research thread also includes spectro-imaging and deep neural networks for EEG classification, and topological data analysis in information space for confidence analysis.
