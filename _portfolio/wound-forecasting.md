## The research question

Can longitudinal biological signals help predict when a chronic wound will heal?

This ongoing research models patient-level wound healing trajectories with Cox time-varying survival models. The goal is to predict healing time from longitudinal wound proteomics data while preserving the changing risk signals observed throughout treatment.

## Why it is difficult

Biomedical forecasting combines limited cohorts with variation across patients, time-dependent covariates, censoring, and irregular changes over time. A convincing evaluation therefore needs to preserve temporal order, handle incomplete outcomes correctly, and expose uncertainty rather than relying on one headline metric.
